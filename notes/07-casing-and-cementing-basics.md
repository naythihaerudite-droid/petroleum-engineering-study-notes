# Casing and Cementing Basics

## Why casing is needed

Casing is steel pipe installed in the wellbore to maintain structural integrity, isolate formations, and support safe drilling and production operations.

## Main purposes of casing

- prevent hole collapse
- isolate freshwater zones
- isolate pressure zones
- support well control
- provide a path for completion equipment
- protect the well over its life cycle

## Common casing strings

### Conductor casing
Used near the surface to support shallow formations and help start the well.

### Surface casing
Protects shallow groundwater and provides a foundation for well control equipment.

### Intermediate casing
Used when extra isolation is needed between surface and production sections.

### Production casing
Installed across the producing zone to support long-term production operations.

## What cementing does

After casing is run, cement is pumped into the annulus between casing and formation.

Main functions of cement:
- provide zonal isolation
- support casing
- protect casing from corrosion in some cases
- prevent fluid movement behind pipe

## Good cementing is important because

Poor cementing can lead to:
- channeling
- unwanted fluid migration
- loss of zonal isolation
- safety and production problems

## Quick review

- Casing provides mechanical support and isolation.
- Cementing helps seal the space outside casing.
- Together, casing and cementing are essential for well integrity.
