# Artificial Lift Overview

## Why artificial lift is used

Artificial lift is needed when the natural reservoir energy is not enough to bring fluids to the surface at an economical rate.

## Common methods

### Sucker rod pump
A rod string drives a downhole pump. This is common in many onshore oil wells.

### Electric submersible pump (ESP)
An electric motor drives a pump installed downhole. ESPs are often used for high liquid rates.

### Gas lift
Gas is injected into the tubing to reduce fluid density and improve lifting efficiency.

### Plunger lift
A plunger helps lift liquids in gas wells and can be useful in certain lower-rate situations.

## Selection depends on

- reservoir pressure
- fluid properties
- depth
- production rate
- sand or solids risk
- power availability
- economics

## Quick review

- Artificial lift is chosen when natural flow is weak.
- Different methods suit different well conditions.
- Selection is both a technical and economic decision.
