# Production Basics

## What is production engineering?

Production engineering focuses on how hydrocarbons move from the reservoir into the well and then to the surface safely and efficiently.

## Main production system parts

- reservoir
- perforations
- tubing
- wellhead
- flowlines
- separators
- surface processing equipment

## Inflow and outflow

### Inflow
Inflow describes fluid entering the wellbore from the reservoir.

### Outflow
Outflow describes fluid moving from the wellbore to the surface through tubing and surface equipment.

A producing well depends on both reservoir deliverability and the pressure losses in the production system.

## Artificial lift

When natural reservoir energy is not enough, artificial lift may be needed. Common methods include:
- sucker rod pump
- electric submersible pump (ESP)
- gas lift
- plunger lift
- hydraulic pumping systems

## Common production issues

- water production / high water cut
- gas coning
- sand production
- scale deposition
- wax deposition
- corrosion
- pressure decline

## Well performance monitoring

Production engineers often monitor:
- oil rate
- gas rate
- water rate
- flowing pressure
- tubing pressure
- temperature
- gas-oil ratio
- water cut

## Production optimization

Optimization may involve:
- changing choke settings
- selecting artificial lift
- stimulating the well
- removing restrictions
- controlling solids or unwanted fluids
- workover operations

## Quick review

- Production engineering connects reservoir behaviour to surface delivery.
- Artificial lift is used when natural energy is not enough.
- Good production performance depends on monitoring, diagnosis, and optimization.
