# Introduction to Petroleum Engineering

## What is petroleum engineering?

Petroleum engineering is the branch of engineering focused on finding, evaluating, drilling, producing, and managing oil and gas resources. It combines geology, physics, chemistry, mathematics, and engineering design.

The main goal is to produce hydrocarbons safely, efficiently, and economically.

## Main branches

### 1. Reservoir engineering
Reservoir engineers study how oil, gas, and water behave inside porous rock. They estimate reserves, predict production, and recommend ways to improve recovery.

### 2. Drilling engineering
Drilling engineers design and supervise wells. They select well paths, drilling fluids, casing programs, and drilling procedures while managing safety and cost.

### 3. Production engineering
Production engineers focus on getting hydrocarbons from the reservoir to the surface in a stable and economical way. They work on tubing, artificial lift, well performance, and flow assurance.

### 4. Petrophysics / formation evaluation
This area deals with understanding rock and fluid properties from logs, cores, and tests. It helps identify whether a formation contains producible hydrocarbons.

## Core ideas every beginner should know

- A reservoir is not an underground lake. It is rock that contains pores filled with fluids.
- Oil and gas flow because of pressure differences.
- Not all hydrocarbons in place can be produced.
- Well design strongly affects safety and productivity.
- Data from geology, logs, cores, tests, and production must be used together.

## Basic workflow of a petroleum project

1. Explore a basin or prospect.
2. Evaluate whether hydrocarbons are present.
3. Drill a well.
4. Test and complete the well.
5. Produce and monitor performance.
6. Improve recovery if possible.
7. Abandon the well safely at the end of field life.

## Important priorities

Petroleum engineering is not only about production. It also requires:
- safety
- environmental responsibility
- cost control
- equipment integrity
- teamwork across disciplines

## Quick review

- Petroleum engineering turns subsurface resources into usable production.
- The main technical areas are reservoir, drilling, production, and formation evaluation.
- Safe and efficient decision-making depends on good data and engineering judgment.
