# Study Roadmap for Beginners

## Stage 1: Build the foundation
Start with:
1. introduction to petroleum engineering
2. reservoir basics
3. drilling basics
4. production basics
5. well logging basics

Goal: understand the major branches and core terminology.

## Stage 2: Understand system behaviour
Then move to:
1. reservoir drive mechanisms
2. casing and cementing basics
3. drilling mud basics

Goal: understand how wells stay safe and how production depends on pressure, fluids, and engineering design.

## Stage 3: Strengthen technical vocabulary
- review the glossary
- review English–Burmese vocabulary
- write short summaries in your own words

## Suggested study habit
- read one note at a time
- make a small keyword list
- explain the concept aloud in simple English
- review again after one or two days

## Long-term idea
Once the basics are clear, the next step is to study:
- well completion
- enhanced oil recovery
- surface facilities
- production optimization
- reservoir simulation

## Reminder
A strong beginner does not try to memorize everything at once. It is better to understand the system clearly first, then add more detail.
