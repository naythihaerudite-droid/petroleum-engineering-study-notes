# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in improving these study notes.

## Ways to contribute

You can help by:
- correcting unclear wording
- suggesting better definitions
- adding simple worked examples
- improving technical accuracy
- expanding bilingual vocabulary support
- proposing new beginner topics

## Suggested contribution style

Please keep notes:
- beginner-friendly
- concise
- practical
- clearly organized
